Policies & Shipping Information

📦 Processing Time

Each item is handmade to order. Please allow 3-5 business days for your order to be created before it is shipped. Processing times may be slightly longer during holidays, sales, or periods of high demand.

🚚 Shipping Policy

Orders are shipped through USPS. Once your order has been shipped, you will receive a shipping confirmation email with your tracking number. Delivery times vary based on your selected shipping method and location.

Due to the handmade and custom nature of our products, returns and exchanges are not accepted. If there is an issue with your order, please contact us within 7 days of delivery and we will work with you to make it right.
