My Story

What started as a creative hobby quickly grew into a passion for designing handmade crochet pieces that are both stylish and unique. Every item in my shop is carefully crafted with attention to detail, quality materials, and a love for creativity.

At Stitched By Trae, I believe handmade items tell a story. Whether you're purchasing a beanie, bucket hat, scrunchie, or another crochet creation, each piece is made with care and designed to help you express your personal style.

I love experimenting with colors, textures, and custom designs to bring your ideas to life and create pieces that feel uniquely yours.
