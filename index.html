About Me

Hi, I'm Tracie, the creator behind Stitched By Trae! 🧶

What started as a creative hobby quickly grew into a passion for designing handmade crochet pieces that are both stylish and unique. Every item in my shop is carefully crafted with attention to detail, quality materials, and a love for creativity.

At Stitched By Trae, I believe handmade items tell a story. Whether you're purchasing a beanie, scrunchie, custom order, or another crochet creation, each piece is made with care and designed to help you express your personal style.

One thing that makes my beanies special is that they're designed with all hair types in mind. As someone who has locs myself, I understand the importance of finding accessories that fit comfortably without sacrificing style. Whether you have locs, braids, curls, natural hair, or straight hair, my beanies are created to be both fashionable and functional.

I love experimenting with colors, textures, and custom designs to bring your ideas to life and create pieces that feel uniquely yours.

And there's more to come! I'm excited to share that handmade crochet bonnets will be coming soon, giving you even more ways to protect and celebrate your hair in style.

Thank you for supporting my small business and allowing me to share my passion with you. Every order, share, and recommendation means the world to me and helps me continue doing what I love.

With love,
Trae
Founder, Stitched By Trae

Policies & Shipping Information

📦 Processing Time

Each item is handmade to order. Please allow 3-5 business days for your order to be created before it is shipped. Processing times may be slightly longer during holidays, sales, or periods of high demand.

🚚 Shipping Policy

Orders are shipped through USPS. Once your order has been shipped, you will receive a shipping confirmation email with your tracking number. Delivery times vary based on your selected shipping method and location.

💜 Return Policy

Due to the handmade and custom nature of our products, returns and exchanges are not accepted. If there is an issue with your order, please contact us within 7 days of delivery and we will work with you to make it right.
